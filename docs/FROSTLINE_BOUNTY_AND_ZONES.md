# Visual bounties and battle zones

The mission panel uses large inline SVG icons, numeric progress and rewards. Only the named bounty target requires a text label. Active real players are preferred; bots are used when no human target is available. A matching confirmed kill grants the pictured rare melee stack and coins once. Bot targets include their spawn generation; NPC award metadata carries that identity through both relay adapters. Disconnected or defeated targets are replaced after a grace period. The sprint button also uses an SVG bolt with a numeric cooldown and accessible label.

The safe circle follows the shared room elapsed clock. Each phase has a 45-second warning and 60-second smooth shrink, with nested, seeded destination circles. Radius goes from 8,000 to 650 over six phases, then remains at the final circle. Outside damage is 3 + 2 × phase HP per second, independent of ordinary hit invulnerability. Players receive phase announcements, visible current/next borders and a direction indicator. Bots take the same zone damage on the elected authority and prioritize moving inside.

Red Zones replace the previous stage-2 player-targeted meteors. A seeded large danger region appears every 60 seconds after the first 30 seconds, warns for five seconds, then schedules four fixed ground strikes per second for twenty seconds. Each strike has 1.25 seconds of warning and a 100-unit blast radius. Moving away from the marked point avoids damage. Impacts trigger layered fire/smoke/debris explosions and temporary craters. Catch-up is bounded to four strikes per update; joining mid-event does not replay its entire history. The schedule and locations derive from the shared room time; they are not independently targeted at each client. Players calculate their own hazard damage, and only the elected authority applies damage to shared NPCs. This is the existing peer-authority architecture, not a dedicated anti-cheat game server.

Validation includes named PvP bounty rewards and duplicate kill delivery, relay forwarding of bot identity/generation, smooth monotonic and nested circles, correct outside damage, deterministic Red Zone points, advance warning, dodging blasts, rapid strikes and effect rendering. Desktop and mobile screenshots are captured by the browser suite. Physical multi-device deployment remains a separate verification step; the full EdgeOne build including its API must be deployed.
