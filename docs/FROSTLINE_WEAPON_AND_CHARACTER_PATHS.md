# Weapon progression and character skills

The shop now lists eight sequential permanent weapon tiers alongside durability upgrades. The path ends at RPG (`ars30`); each purchase unlocks the next step, rather than buying one gun or increasing everyone else's loot tier. Prices start at 40 coins and rise by 1.7 per tier. Purchases apply next round, preserving a consistent active loadout/match band.

On weapon pickup the collector receives their unlocked tier's weapon. At base tier ordinary melee loot retains its variety; firearm loot becomes a base knife. Higher tiers convert weapon pickups into the selected tier, preserving identity/rarity but deriving attack statistics from the new weapon type. This allows several RPGs to be collected at tier eight. The shared drop is still claimed once; conversion uses a copy and does not change another player's tier or the shared ground record. The ground preview shows the local collector's resulting weapon. Quantity pickups and legacy purchased starting equipment also use the collector's progression. The visible orbit limit remains 60.

Fifteen characters now have distinct pairs of existing run perks. A new round grants one rank of each; level gains automatically alternate their priorities until maxed, then use other available perks. There is no three-card skill prompt or pending-choice button. Skill descriptions appear in the character shop. The skill identity is fixed for the round, so changing appearance mid-run cannot farm starting bonuses. All characters receive equal numbers of starting ranks; paid characters differ in build, not in the number of starting skills.

Existing coins, durability tiers, owned skins and legacy equipment records are preserved. The new weapon tier defaults to one for existing saves and is stored independently from durability. Match grouping uses the higher of the two permanent tiers. World networking, pickup ownership and existing fifty-plus integration checks remain in the suite; balance between character builds still requires human playtesting.
